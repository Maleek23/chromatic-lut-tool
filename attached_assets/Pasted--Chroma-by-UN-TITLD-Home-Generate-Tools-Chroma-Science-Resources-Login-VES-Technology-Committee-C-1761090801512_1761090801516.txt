CIE L*a*b* Transform
Perceptually Uniform Color Space

Transforms RGB colors into the CIE L*a*b* color space, ensuring perceptually uniform color processing.

Why This Matters

RGB values don't match human perception. L*a*b* ensures equal mathematical changes produce equal visual changes.

Technical Implementation:

→
Converts from device-dependent RGB to perceptual L*a*b*
→
Lightness (L*) separated from color (a*, b*)
→
Enables accurate skin tone detection and protection
→
Foundation for professional color grading

Skin Tone Protection
Selective Color Preservation

Detects and protects skin tone ranges to prevent unnatural color shifts in portraits and people.

Why This Matters

Aggressive color grading can make skin tones look alien. Protection ensures natural, flattering skin across all ethnicities.

Technical Implementation:

→
Identifies skin tone vector in L*a*b* space
→
Protects hues from 15°-50° (skin tone line)
→
Works across all skin tones and ethnicities
→
Prevents unnatural green/magenta casts
